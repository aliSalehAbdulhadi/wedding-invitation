Observed direction: burgundy envelope and champagne wax seal; peach stationery; floral corners and fine arch ornament; paired swans; centered calligraphy; restrained vertically arranged schedule. No reference assets are copied.

Plan: peach paper #fbf1e8, ivory #fffbf5, burgundy ink #4b3034, wine #683b43, muted sage #bbb392, champagne #b89768. Preserve Aref Ruqaa for Arabic names, Cormorant italic ampersand and English names, Noto Sans Arabic for details. An illustrated garden arch frames the central names and numeric date; the burgundy envelope supplies the strongest opening moment. Existing narrative sections, bird, door opening, dance, maps, calendar, personal PNG, public themes and song-only half-volume audio stay functional.

The Rose & silk palette is the branch default; existing guest palette choices remain available. The native hero text remains selectable and bilingual, separate from artwork. The illustration uses responsive transparent WebP files with fixed dimensions and no continuous image animation.

Original illustration produced with the built-in image-generation tool. Final prompt: a transparent portrait watercolor antique garden arch in champagne engraving, dusty blush roses, ivory ranunculus, pale butter blossoms and sage stems; airy asymmetric upper corners, fuller lower corners, two ivory swans at the bottom, central 65 percent empty for live text, no lettering or brand assets. Source PNG stays outside the repository. Published asset paths: public/assets/images/romantic-garden-640.webp and romantic-garden-1024.webp.

This is an independent branch exploration, not a change to production main.

The study uses its own wedding-romantic-palette-v1 preference key so trying these colors locally does not overwrite the production edition preference. The frame has one brief opacity/transform entrance, no ambient bitmap loop. Responsive assets are 281,210 and 512,672 bytes; the 640px version is selected at standard-density phone and desktop widths.
