## Final generation prompt

Create a refined final wedding sharing image, landscape composition, intended 1200 by 630 pixels, flat solid warm ivory background #f7f5ee. Center one custom, unified Arabic calligraphic monogram in deep olive #293e32. The only letters are the Arabic initial ع (ayn, for علي / Ali) and ت (taa, for تبارك / Tabarak). This must be a newly designed single ligature, NOT two typeset letters next to each other and NOT two overlapping font characters. Co-design the mark so the open head and descending stroke of ع flows seamlessly into one shared sweeping bowl that also expresses ت; the recognizable pair of taa dots sits elegantly above that shared bowl. Use controlled Diwani-inspired pen contrast, a single coherent calligraphic rhythm, balanced negative space and restrained flourishes. Both letter identities must remain correct and legible to an Arabic reader. It should feel like a personal bespoke wedding insignia made by a skilled Arabic calligrapher. Keep the mark compact, balanced and highly polished, about 28 percent of image width and 55 percent of height, surrounded by generous clean ivory negative space. Flat vector-like edges, one ink color, no shadows, gradients, texture, 3D, mockups, circles, borders, flowers, hearts, additional lettering, names, numerals, captions, watermarks or presentation grid. Deliver just the clean final monogram sharing artwork.

The tool returned 1730 x 909; metadata uses those actual dimensions.
